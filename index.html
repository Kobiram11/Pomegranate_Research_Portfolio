R26-IT-087 IT4010 Research Project · 2026

An AI-Based Intelligent Farming System for Improving Pomegranate Yield and Quality

An integrated IoT and image-based machine learning system that supports pomegranate cultivation decisions, from soil monitoring to post-harvest fruit grading.

Department of Information Technology · Faculty of Computing · Sri Lanka Institute of Information Technology (SLIIT)

Abstract

Pomegranate farmers in Sri Lanka often make cultivation decisions without clear, timely information. Soil conditions are guessed, diseases are noticed late, fruit growth stages are estimated by eye, and fruit grading after harvest is done manually. This group research develops an integrated AI-based intelligent farming system that combines IoT soil sensing and image-based machine learning models. The system is organised into four individual components: fruit growth stage detection and harvest time prediction, fruit disease detection and treatment recommendation, fruit quality grading and waste utilization analysis, and intelligent fertilizer recommendation with weather-informed irrigation decisions.

Research overview

Problem area

Farmers depend on manual practices, have limited knowledge of soil, irrigation and nutrient levels, detect diseases late, and lack tools to monitor fruit growth or grade fruit consistently.

Approach

An integrated system that uses IoT sensors for soil data and image-based deep learning for fruit analysis, supporting decisions from soil monitoring to post-harvest grading.

Expected contribution

Clear, component-specific decision support that aims to reduce crop loss, reduce fruit waste and encourage careful use of water and fertilizer.

Literature survey

IoT soil and weather monitoring

IoT systems have been used to monitor soil moisture, NPK levels, pH and temperature for pomegranate cultivation and to send readings to a dashboard or phone. These systems mainly display raw values and give limited action advice.

Sources: [1] [2]

Fertilizer recommendation

Studies apply supervised learning such as Random Forest, support vector machines, logistic regression, XGBoost and regression models to recommend fertilizer types, NPK quantities or mixing ratios. These targets differ from one another and cannot be compared directly.

Sources: [3] [5] [6]

Irrigation advice and control

IoT and cloud-based irrigation systems combine soil moisture sensors with weather prediction APIs or remote motor control. Irrigation decisions are often based only on soil moisture or humidity.

Sources: [4] [7]

Image-based disease detection

Image processing and CNN models have been used to identify pomegranate diseases. Most studies focus on leaf disease detection rather than fruit disease detection.

Sources: [8] [9]

Fruit growth, grading and waste use

Image-based monitoring of fruit growth and harvest time has been studied, but growth stage monitoring is rarely included in farming systems. Fruit grading research mostly focuses on grading accuracy, while pomegranate peels and seeds can be used for food, cosmetic and other value-added products.

Sources: [10] [11]

Integrated pomegranate platforms

Some systems combine nutrient, climate, disease and fruit quality functions, using Random Forest for structured data and CNNs for image tasks. Many other systems address a single agricultural problem only.

Sources: [12]

Research gap

  • Many research systems address a single agricultural problem, such as leaf disease detection, soil monitoring or post-harvest grading.
  • Most disease detection studies analyse leaves rather than pomegranate fruit.
  • Soil monitoring systems often display parameters without fertilizer or irrigation recommendations, and irrigation advice is rarely linked with rainfall forecasts.
  • Fruit growth stage monitoring and harvest time prediction are rarely considered.
  • Fruit grading studies focus on accuracy and do not recommend how each quality level should be used.

Research problem

  • Pomegranate farmers in Sri Lanka still depend largely on traditional, manual farming practices, while Sri Lanka spends a large amount on pomegranate imports each year.
  • Farmers lack clear knowledge of soil conditions, irrigation needs and nutrient levels, which can lead to overuse or underuse of water and fertilizer.
  • Fruit diseases such as Bacterial Blight, Anthracnose and Alternaria are often detected late, causing crop damage and yield loss.
  • There is no practical way to monitor fruit growth stages or predict harvest time, and manual grading after harvest leads to fruit waste.

Research objectives

Main objective

To develop an integrated AI-based intelligent farming system that combines IoT sensors and image-based machine learning models to support pomegranate farmers from soil monitoring to post-harvest fruit grading.

Specific objectives

  1. Detect pomegranate fruit growth stages from images and predict the time remaining to harvest.
  2. Detect and classify pomegranate fruit diseases from images and recommend suitable treatments.
  3. Grade fruit into High, Medium and Low quality and recommend suitable usage for each level.
  4. Collect soil readings with a portable IoT device, recommend fertilizer levels using soil data and tree age, and support irrigation decisions using soil moisture and weather information.

Methodology

  1. Problem studyReview literature, existing systems and farmer practices, supported by discussions with pomegranate farmers.
  2. Data collectionCollect fruit images from public datasets and farm sources, and soil readings from a portable 7-in-1 soil sensor together with weather data.
  3. PreprocessingClean, resize and label images; clean structured soil and weather records and prepare model features.
  4. Model developmentTrain deep learning models (CNN, YOLO) for image tasks and Random Forest classifiers for structured soil and weather data.
  5. System developmentBuild application interfaces for uploading images, receiving sensor readings and displaying results and recommendations.
  6. EvaluationEvaluate each component separately with training and testing splits and classification metrics, then validate with new real-world inputs.

Research components and responsibilities

Each component is an individual research contribution with its own inputs, methods and evaluation.

Fruit Growth Stage Detection and Harvest Time Prediction

An AI-based component that detects pomegranate fruit growth stages from images and predicts the remaining days to harvest.

Responsible: Thishoharini V · IT22281296
Problem
  • Farmers usually estimate fruit maturity manually.
  • Incorrect harvesting can lead to poor fruit quality and market loss.
  • Pomegranate fruits pass through several growth stages that are difficult to identify accurately.
Objectives
  • Collect a pomegranate growth stage image dataset.
  • Apply image preprocessing techniques.
  • Train a deep learning model to classify five growth stages.
  • Predict harvest readiness from the detected stage.
  • Evaluate performance using accuracy and classification metrics.
Method

A farmer uploads a fruit image. The image is preprocessed, YOLO detects the fruit, a CNN classifies the growth stage, and the system estimates the time remaining to harvest. Weather conditions can adjust the prediction.

Technologies
  • YOLO
  • CNN
  • OpenCV
  • TensorFlow / Keras
  • Python
Evaluation

Training and testing split; accuracy and classification metrics; validation with new fruit images.

Fruit Disease Detection and Treatment Recommendation

An AI-based component that detects and classifies pomegranate fruit diseases from images and provides treatment recommendations.

Responsible: Banuja S · IT22188304
Problem
  • Fruit diseases reduce fruit quality and crop yield.
  • Farmers mostly rely on manual visual inspection, and early symptoms are hard to identify.
  • Most existing studies focus on leaf disease rather than fruit disease.
Objectives
  • Collect healthy and diseased pomegranate fruit images.
  • Preprocess images to improve quality.
  • Develop and train a disease detection model (Healthy, Bacterial Blight, Anthracnose, Alternaria).
  • Design a web-based system to upload images, display results and suggest treatment.
Method

A farmer uploads a fruit image. The image is preprocessed and a CNN identifies whether the fruit is healthy or diseased. If a disease is detected, its type is identified and a treatment is suggested.

Technologies
  • CNN
  • TensorFlow / Keras
  • OpenCV
  • Python
Evaluation

Model accuracy (proposal target: around 90% or higher), farm testing with uploaded fruit images, and response time.

Results

To be confirmed

Fruit Quality Grading and Waste Utilization Analysis

An AI-based web component that grades pomegranate fruit quality and recommends suitable usage for each grade.

Responsible: Sowmiya A · IT22549440
Problem
  • Many farmers sort fruit manually after harvest, which is slow and not always accurate.
  • Low-quality fruits are often thrown away as waste.
  • Existing research focuses on grading accuracy without recommending how graded fruit should be used.
Objectives
  • Collect and prepare a pomegranate fruit image dataset.
  • Apply image preprocessing techniques.
  • Train ML/DL models to classify High, Medium and Low quality.
  • Develop a web system to show grading results and usage recommendations (export, processing or fertilizer).
Method

A fruit image is uploaded, preprocessed and its features extracted. The model classifies quality, and the system displays the grade with a recommended usage.

Technologies
  • CNN
  • SVM and ANN (for comparison)
  • OpenCV
  • TensorFlow / Keras
  • Python
Evaluation

80% training and 20% testing split; CNN, SVM and ANN compared; classification accuracy (proposal target: about 90%); testing with real fruit images.

Results

Intelligent Fertilizer Recommendation and Irrigation Decision System

Thesis title: IoT Based Smart Fertilizer and Weather Aware Irrigation System for Pomegranate Cultivation

An IoT and machine learning component that uses soil readings, tree age and weather information to support fertilizer and irrigation decisions.

Responsible: Kobiram T · IT22245656
Problem
  • Soil information, crop age and weather information are often available separately and are not interpreted together for a decision.
  • Irrigation is commonly based only on soil moisture, without considering expected rainfall.
  • Laboratory soil testing is slow and not real-time.
Objectives
  • Collect soil N, P, K, pH, moisture and temperature with a portable 7-in-1 soil sensor, ESP32 and RS485 to TTL module.
  • Transfer readings to the mobile application through Bluetooth.
  • Combine the six soil readings with manually entered tree age to classify Low, Medium or High fertilizer levels, with Urea, TSP and MOP information.
  • Combine soil moisture with weather data for the farm location to recommend one of four irrigation action states.
  • Evaluate both classifiers using separate test data.
Method

Readings travel from the sensor to the ESP32 and then to the mobile app through Bluetooth. Tree age is converted into a cultivation category, forming a seven-feature input for a Random Forest fertilizer classifier. A separate Random Forest classifier combines moisture with Open-Meteo weather data to choose an irrigation state: Suitable to irrigate, Skip – rain expected, Skip – soil already wet, or No urgent irrigation.

Technologies
  • ESP32
  • 7-in-1 soil sensor
  • RS485 to TTL
  • Bluetooth
  • Random Forest
  • Open-Meteo API
  • Flutter / Dart
  • TensorFlow Lite
  • Python
Evaluation

Fertilizer: stratified 80/20 split with five-fold cross validation. Irrigation: chronological split. Accuracy, precision, recall and F1-score.

Results

Recorded in the individual thesis: fertilizer test accuracy 95.18% (mean five-fold cross validation 96.54%) and irrigation test accuracy 91.85%. These values measure agreement with reference labels; they do not establish fertilizer quantity accuracy, sensor calibration, water savings or yield improvement.

This component does not perform image-based growth monitoring, disease prediction or fruit quality prediction.

Technologies used

  • PythonData preparation and model development
  • TensorFlow / KerasTraining deep learning models
  • Convolutional Neural Networks (CNN)Image classification of growth stage, disease and quality
  • YOLODetecting pomegranate fruits in images
  • OpenCVImage preprocessing and feature extraction
  • Random ForestFertilizer level and irrigation state classification
  • ESP32 and 7-in-1 soil sensorPortable soil sensing (N, P, K, pH, moisture, temperature)
  • RS485 to TTL module and BluetoothSensor communication and transfer of readings to the mobile application
  • Open-Meteo weather APIWeather information for irrigation decisions
  • Flutter / Dart and TensorFlow LiteMobile application and on-device inference
